Match formula, as filed

During 2023, the Company provided a discretionary match of 50% of the first 6% of base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan, excluding Nortek Global HVAC LLC Union employees.

Vesting, as filed

A Participant is 34% vested in the Company’s discretionary matching contributions after one year of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 33% until 100% vested after three years of credited service.
