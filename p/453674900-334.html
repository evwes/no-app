Match formula, as filed

The Corporation's contribution is based on matching 100% of the first 3% of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan plus 50% of the next 2% of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Certain eligible participants that worked at least 1,000 hours and were employed as of the end of the plan year receive nonelective Corporation contributions equal to 3.5% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are fully vested in the Corporation's nonelective contributions plus actual earnings after three years of credited service.
