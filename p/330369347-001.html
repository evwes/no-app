Match formula, as filed

The Company makes safe harbor matching contributions at 100% of the first 3% of compensation deferred by eligible employees plus 50% of the next 3% deferred, up to a maximum Company contribution of 4.5% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting of Company contributions plus earnings thereon is based on years of service at a rate of 25% per year, except that safe harbor matching contributions are immediately 100% vested.
