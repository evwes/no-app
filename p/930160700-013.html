Match formula, as filed

For participants who are employees of the Company, the amount of matching contributions is equal to 50% of the participants’ first 6% of before-tax or Roth contributions, up to a maximum of 3% of the employee’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Profit sharing contributions for all employees and employer-matching contributions for those employees hired after October 1, 2002, vest at the rate of 20% per year over a five-year period.
