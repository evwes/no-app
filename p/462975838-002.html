Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2025, the Plan was amended to provide for safe harbor matching employer contributions at a rate of 100% of the first 4% of eligible participant's compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Additionally, participants merged in from Village MD 401(k) Plan were 50% vested in their qualified automatic contribution arrangement contributions after one year of credit service and became 100% vested after two years of credit service.
