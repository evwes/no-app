Match formula, as filed

The Company may contribute to the Plan a matching contribution equal to 50% of the first 6% of a participant’s annual compensation contributed as a pre-tax contribution, limited to 3% of a participant’s annual compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in Company contributions is based on years of service, increasing 20% each year with 100% vesting after five years of service.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Hexcel Corporation Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 53.1% of the trust's $599 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
