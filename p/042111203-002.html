Match formula, as filed

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 10% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Prior to January I, 2025, The University contributed 10% of a participant's base pay as long as the participant was contributing at least 5% of their base pay to a DC account.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting - Participants are immediately 100% vested in all contributions and earnings allocated to their accounts.
