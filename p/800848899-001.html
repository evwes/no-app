Match formula, as filed

In 2025, the Companies contributed, in the form of a matching contribution, an amount equal to 100 percent of each participant’s eligible contribution, up to the first 6 percent of eligible compensation for participants defined as non-Project Hires.

Vesting, as filed

Participants with less than one year of service as of December 31, 2025 became immediately eligible for matching contributions, while participants hired on or after January 1, 2026 are subject to a one-year cliff vesting requirement for such contributions.
