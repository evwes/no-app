Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 2% of active participants' eligible compensation and 50% of the next 4% of the active participant's eligible compensation, contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Each participant's matching contributions become fully vested upon normal retirement, disability, death, or after two years of service.
