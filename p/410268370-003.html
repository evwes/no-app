Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes Safe Harbor Matching Contributions (“matching contributions”) to employees’ accounts by matching 100 percent of an employee’s contributions, up to 4 percent of the employee’s eligible compensation, to be allocated according to the employee’s elections.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100 percent vested in these contributions after three years of vesting service with the Employer, or upon age 65 (normal retirement date as defined in the Plan), disability, death or plan termination.
