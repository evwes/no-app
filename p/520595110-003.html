Instead, these employees are eligible to receive matching contributions equal to 20% of the first 3% of base salary an eligible participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 6% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

(b) Contributions Prior to July 1, 2011, the University contributed 6% of the eligible participant’s base salary, as defined, up to the age of 35 after two years of service and 12% of base salary for participants aged 35 and older.

Vesting, as filed

(d) Vesting Each participant is fully and immediately 100% vested in their employee and employer account balances.
