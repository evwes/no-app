Match formula, as filed

As of November 1, 2009, all participants are credited with a basic contribution by the Society equal to 6% of compensation and a matching contribution by the Society at a rate of 50% of the participant’s contributions not to exceed 6% of the participant’s compensation under the DC Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants become vested in the Society's basic and matching contributions upon completion of three years of participation in the DC Plan.
