Match formula, as filed

Valero matches 200 percent of the first 3 percent of eligible compensation that a participant contributes on a pre-tax basis or as a Roth 401(k) contribution to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants vest in their employer matching contribution accounts at the rate of 20 percent per year and are 100 percent vested after five years of service.
