Match formula, as filed

For 2025 the discretionary matching percentage was 100% of the first 6% of eligible compensation for eligible participants hired on or after December 2, 2024 and 137% of the first 6% of eligible compensation for those hired before December 2, 2024.

Vesting, as filed

A plan amendment effective August 1, 2026, changed the vesting for employer matching contributions from 100% vesting immediately to 100% vesting after three years of service.
