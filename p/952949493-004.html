Match formula, as filed

The Company shall make Company Matching Contributions to the Plan equal to 100% of the participant's contributions up to 6% of the participant's eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired on or after January 1, 1998 and terminated prior to January 1, 2001 vest in their company matching contributions at a rate of 33'/3% per each year resulting in 100% vesting after 3 years of credited service as defined by the Plan.
