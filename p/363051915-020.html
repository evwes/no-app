Match formula, as filed

Participants eligible for Company matching contributions also receive a separate, discretionary Retirement Account Contribution (RAC) of 2.5% of eligible compensation if they are actively employed on the last day of the year, regardless of whether participant contributions are made.

Vesting, as filed

Participants will be 100% vested in Company contributions upon earning two years of service (if an employee terminates employment before earning two years of service, Company contributions will be forfeited).
