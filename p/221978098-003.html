Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions for participants equal to 50% of participant deferrals (excluding catch- up and after-tax contributions) up to 6% of compensation. Effec

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Effective January 1, 2019, the Company may make non-elective contributions equal to 5% of eligible compensation for participants hired on and after July 1, 2018.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become fully vested in the portion of their accounts attributed to the Company's contributions after completing three years of service, as defined in the Plan Document.
