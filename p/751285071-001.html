Match formula, as filed

Before January 01, 2024, for employees covered under the core Plan or specific collective bargaining agreements, the Company made a fixed matching contribution equal to 30% of participant contributions (excluding catch-up contributions), up to 6% of the employee’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Effective January 1, 2024, matching and nonelective contributions and earnings, vest according to a 5-year graded vesting schedule (0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 100%) beginning after two years of service for employees covered under the core Plan.
