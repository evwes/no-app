Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The non-elective company contribution is a 2% non-elective company contribution based on eligible earnings to all eligible employees, subject to 415(c) annual limits.

Vesting, as filed

This non-elective contribution and any associated earnings will have a three-year cliff vesting for new hires on or after January 1, 2022.
