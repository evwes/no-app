Match formula, as filed

For employees of LCS-Westminster Partnership IV LLP and Heritage Retirement Facilities LLC, the Employer provides a matching contribution equal to 50% of the participant’s eligible contributions, subject to a maximum of 3% of eligible compensation for active employees contributing to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

For employees of Heritage Retirement Facilities LLC and LCS- Westminster Partnership IV LLP, contributions become 20% vested after two years of credited service, as defined, and continue to vest at the rate of 20% for each successive year of service until 100% vested after six years of service.
