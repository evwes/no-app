Match formula, as filed

For both the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, the Company made matching contributions equal to 50% of the participant’s deferrals up to the first 6% of the participant’s compensation for employees hired after June 1, 2009.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting begins after the first year of service, and a participant is 100% vested after five years of credited service.
