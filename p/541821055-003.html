Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2017, the Company’s matching contribution to the Plan is equal to 100% of the employee’s contributions up to 6% of the employee’s pretax annual compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Effective January 1, 2017, a 3-year credited service vesting period was eliminated and participants actively employed on or after January 1, 2017, became 100% vested in the Company’s matching contributions and the annual retirement contributions.
