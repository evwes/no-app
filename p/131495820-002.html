Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for an Employer matching contribution equal to 67% of the deferral contributions up to 6% of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Additionally, the Employer makes a non-elective contribution equal to 2% of each eligible employee’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are also immediately 100% vested in Employer matching contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
