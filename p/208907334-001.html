Match formula, as filed

The Plan allows for Company safe-harbor matching contributions in an amount equal to 100% of the first 3% of a participant's eligible wages and 50% of the next 2% of contributed compensation on a pay period basis.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after six years of credited service.
