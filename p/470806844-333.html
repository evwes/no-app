Match formula, as filed

The Companies make matching contributions of 50% of employees’ salary deferral amounts up to 6% of employees’ compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 50% vested in Company matching contributions after two years of service and 100% vested in Company matching contributions after three years of service or upon death, disability, or retirement.
