Match formula, as filed

For competitive employees hired on or after January 1, 2000, the Company made nonelective contributions of 2% of eligible compensation and matching contributions equal to 60% of the eligible participants’ elective deferral contributions not to exceed 6% of total eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

For competitive employees hired on or after January 1, 2000, the Company made nonelective contributions of 2% of eligible compensation and matching contributions equal to 60% of the eligible participants’ elective deferral contributions not to exceed 6% of total eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

For all other Company contributions to this Plan (nonelective and matching), participants vest at a rate of 20% per year and are fully vested after five years of credited service.
