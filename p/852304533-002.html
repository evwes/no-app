Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for safe harbor matching contributions in an amount equal to 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation that a participant elects to contribute to the Plan, plus 50% of the participant’s contribution between 3% and 5% of their eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are vested immediately in their contributions and safe harbor matching contributions plus earnings and losses thereon.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Wellpower 401(K) Savings And Retirement Plan And The Wellpower Hourly 401(K) Plan Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 39.6% of the trust's $231 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
