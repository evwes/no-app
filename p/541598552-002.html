Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2025, the Company provides safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of a participant’s deferrals up to 3% of eligible compensation, plus 50% of a participant’s deferrals that exceed 3% but do not exceed 6% of eligible compensation.
