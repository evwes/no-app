Match formula, as filed

The Company makes an eligible automatic contribution arrangement (EACA) matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 4% of compensation contributed by the participant and 50% of the next 2% of compensation contributed by the participant.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after two years of service where they worked at least 1,000 hours each year, including EACA matching and non-elective contributions made after January 1, 2016 for participants transferred in from a merged plan.
