Match formula, as filed

On January 1, 2017, the Plan was amended to adopt the safe harbor matching contribution provisions of the Internal Revenue Code (IRC), which provides for Employer Safe Harbor Matching Contributions of 100% of the first 5% of participants' contributions (Safe Harbor Matching Contributions).

Vesting, as filed

As a result of this amendment, all regular matching contributions for participants employed on January 1, 2017 and all Safe Harbor Matching Contributions are 100% vested immediately.
