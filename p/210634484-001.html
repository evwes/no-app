Match formula, as filed

Employer Contributions The Organization makes a matching contribution of 50% of the participant's eligible contributions up to of 4% of plan compensation, resulting in a maximum matching contribution of 2% of plan compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Organization makes a nonelective contribution equal to 4% of plan compensation for eligible participants employed by Inspira Medical Centers, Inc.

Vesting, as filed

Employer contributions are subject to a six-year graded vesting schedule.
