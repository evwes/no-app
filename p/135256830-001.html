Match formula, as filed

The Company makes safe harbor matching contributions which are equal to 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation deferred by the employee, and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation deferred by the employee.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, the Company makes fixed nonelective contributions equal to 3% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their deferral, rollover and Company safe harbor matching contributions, including the earnings on those amounts.
