Match formula, as filed

The Plan includes an automatic contribution arrangement and provides for safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of the first 5% of eligible compensation contributed by each participant to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.
