Match formula, as filed

For all eligible active participants, the Company contributes a matching contribution equal to 33.3% of the participant’s year to date contribution, up to 2% of the participant’s year to date eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Employer basic contributions and employer matching contributions made on behalf of employees are subject to a six-year graded vesting schedule at a rate of 20% per year, commencing with the completion of two years of service.
