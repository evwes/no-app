Match formula, as filed

Employer matching contributions are based on each participant's 401(k) Component contributions, up to 6% of eligible compensation, and are made each pay period in the amount of 50% of these contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

PIA The Company contributes an amount equal to 5% of eligible compensation to those participants electing the PIA.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired prior to January 1, 2016, vest in Company contributions under the 401(k) Component, ESOP, PIA and MCC, including earnings and losses thereon, at a rate of 20% per year and become fully vested in all Company matching contributions after three years.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Medtronic, Inc. Master Trust Fund. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 85.2% of the trust's $13.8 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
