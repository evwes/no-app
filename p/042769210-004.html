Match formula, as filed

The Health System currently makes an employer matching contribution of 50% of the first 6% of the participant’s salary deferral contributions up to a maximum of 3% of compensation for the participants that meet the allocation requirements.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The nonelective contribution credited is based on a scale of 1.5% to 2.5% for the year ended December 31, 2025 and 2% to 3% for the year ended December 31, 2024 of covered pay, based on the employee’s age and years of service.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
