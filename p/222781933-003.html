Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Owens-Illinois, Inc. Master Savings Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 67.5% of the trust's $702 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
