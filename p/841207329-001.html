Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a non-discretionary matching contribution on behalf of each participant equal to 50% of the participants’ contributions and up to 10% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 12% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Effective January 1, 2024, the Company will contribute a non-discretionary nonelective contribution equal to 12% of each crew member’s compensation during the applicable payroll period for 2024 and 14% beginning January 1, 2025 during the applicable payroll period.

Vesting, as filed

Employer matching and discretionary profit-sharing contributions vest after three years of service.
