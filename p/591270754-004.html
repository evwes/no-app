Match formula, as filed

The Company, at its discretion, matches 100% of the participant’s contributions up to a maximum of $250 per year except for attorneys who are associates or who are of counsel employees and are not eligible for shareholder benefits and are not eligible for employer matching contributions.

Vesting, as filed

Associate attorneys are vested in the portion of their accounts attributable to the Company’s discretionary contributions, if any, at the rate of 20% per year of service.
