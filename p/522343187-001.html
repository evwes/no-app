Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes, as a match, 100% of the employee's deferral percentage, up to 4% of eligible compensation to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Additionally, the Company contributes 4% of eligible compensation to each participant's account.
