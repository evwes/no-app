Match formula, as filed

Employees receive an employer matching contribution equal to the sum of 100% of the portion of participant contributions, which do not exceed 3% of annual compensation, plus 50% of the portion of participant contributions between 3% and 5% of annual compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their individual contributions and employer contributions, plus actual earnings thereon; however, employer contributions may not be distributed for reasons of hardship.
