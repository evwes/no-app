Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Bank makes an automatic non-elective contribution of 4% of base salary (to a maximum base salary of $100,000); and contributes 100% of each participant’s contribution up to the first 3% of base compensation and 50% of each participant’s contribution up to the next 3% of base compensation.
