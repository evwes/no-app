Match formula, as filed

In 2025 and 2024, the Company matched, at the end of each pay period, 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of the participants’ contributions to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

For the plan years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, the Company contributed 3% of a participant’s eligible compensation for a total of $11,863,055 and $13,297,206, respectively, in Supplemental Retirement Contributions to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
