Match formula, as filed

EOG matches pretax and Roth contributions dollar for dollar on the first 6% of considered compensation that an eligible participant contributes to the Plan as a pretax salary deferral and/or a Roth elective deferral.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become 20% vested in their employer matching and employer retirement contributions subaccounts after one year of service and vest an additional 20% for each year of service thereafter.
