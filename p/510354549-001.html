Match formula, as filed

The Company has elected to make a discretionary matching contribution to the Plan for each eligible Participant in an amount equal to 50% of the Participant’s contributions, up to six percent of Compensation per each pay period.

Vesting, as filed

Participants in the Plan prior to January 1, 2018 are 100% vested in employer matching contributions immediately, while Participants joining the Plan on or after January 1, 2018 vest in employer matching contributions 25% per year over a four-year period.
