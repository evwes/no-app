Match formula, as filed

For nonunion employees hired after August 31, 2004, the Company makes a matching contribution of 100% of the first 6% of compensation a participant defers plus 50% of the next 4% a participant contributes for a total matching contribution of 8%.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired or rehired after August 31, 2004 are fully vested in matching contributions after three years of credited service.
