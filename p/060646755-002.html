Match formula, as filed

The Medical Center makes safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of the first 4% of compensation deferred by the participant.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, the Medical Center makes a nonelective core contribution which ranges from 1% to 2% of the participant's compensation, based on a participant's age plus their years of service on the last day of the plan year.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested in the Medical Center's nonelective core contributions and the Medical Center's safe harbor matching contributions after two years of credited service.
