Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a discretionary match, calculated as 50% of the first 6% of base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Employees hired after December 31, 2008, who meet the Plan’s age requirement and have completed 12 months of service, receive a discretionary nonelective contribution of 3% of eligible compensation into their Participant accounts, in addition to being eligible for the matching contribution.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
