Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for nondiscretionary matching contributions equal to 50% of an employee’s before-tax and Roth 401(k) contributions, including catch-up contributions, not to exceed 6% of the employee’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Employees hired after December 31, 2021, are subject to three year cliff vesting for any Company contributions.
