Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for a safe-harbor employer matching contribution (“Matching Contribution”) equal to 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% contributed by the participant.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are fully vested at all times in the portion of their accounts attributable to their elective deferrals, rollover contributions, matching contributions and qualified non-elective contributions.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Xpo Logistics Inc. Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 89.5% of the trust's $2.8 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
