Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions to the Plan equal to 100% of the first 2% and 50% of the next 1% of the contributions made by participating employees, up to a maximum employer contribution of 2.5% of the participants’ eligible compensation, as defined.

Vesting, as filed

All participants are 100% vested after 3 years of service as defined by the Plan.
