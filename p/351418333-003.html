Match formula, as filed

The Company provides a safe harbor matching contribution of 100% of the elective salary deferral amounts on the first 3% of the participant's contribution and an additional 50% of the next 2% of the participant's contribution.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 25% vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 25% until 100% vested after five years of credited service.
