Match formula, as filed

The Employer provides a safe harbor matching contribution of 100% of the elective salary deferral amounts on the first 3% of the participant’s contribution and an additional 50% of the next 2% of the participant’s contribution.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired on or after January 1, 2012, will vest 25% per year beginning on year two and will become fully vested after five years are immediately vested in their contributions, Employer contributions and actual earnings thereon.
