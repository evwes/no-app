Match formula, as filed

The Company will match 100.0% on the first 1.0% of eligible earnings contributed and 50.0% on the next 5.0% of eligible compensation contributed on each participant’s combined pretax, catch-up, Roth, and/or Roth catch-up contributions.

Vesting, as filed

For participants who first perform service for a sponsoring employer on or after January 1, 2008, the vesting schedule for company matching contributions will be as follows: fewer than 12 months – 0%; 12 or more months – 100%.
