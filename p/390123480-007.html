Match formula, as filed

For most corporate employees, Thrivent makes a matching contribution equal to 100% of the participant’s contributions, up to 6% of earnings (specifically, “Considered Compensation”) for a maximum matching contribution of 6%.

Vesting, as filed

in Thrivent’s matching contributions and related investment income or loss at the rate of 20% upon completion of one year of service, and an additional 20% upon completion of each succeeding year of service until fully vested at the end of five years.
