Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes to the Plan a matching safe harbor contribution equal to each participant's salary deferral contributions up to 100 percent of the first 5 percent deferred by the eligible employees.

Vesting, as filed

Participants vest in contributions made by the Company 20 percent after two years of service, an additional 20 percent after the three years of service, and another 20 percent per year thereafter until they become fully vested after six years of service.
