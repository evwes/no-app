Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 100% of the first 1% of eligible before-tax and/or Roth 401(k) participant contributions and 50% of the next 6% of eligible before-tax and/or Roth 401(k) participant contributions each pay period up to a total annual matching contribution of 4% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed
