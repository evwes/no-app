Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes matching contributions equal to 50% of each participant's contribution on the first 10% of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Employer also makes a non-elective contribution equal to 3.5% of eligible compensation for a select group of participants.

Vesting, as filed

All participant and Employer contributions are immediately 100% vested.
