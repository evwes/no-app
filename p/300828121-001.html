Match formula, as filed

The Company’s discretionary matching contribution utilizes a tiered formula consisting of 100% of the first 2% and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation that participants contribute to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 33 percent vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 33 percent until 100 percent vested after four years of credited service.
