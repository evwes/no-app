Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

For most of the participating employers, the Company contributes 5 percent of each participant's compensation up to the Social Security Wage Base (SSWB) and 9 percent of compensation in excess of the SSWB up to the maximum annual compensation allowed under the Plan for 2020, 2019, and 2018.

Vesting, as filed

Holy Spirit matching contributions are vested 20 percent after one year of service, 40 percent after two years of service and 100 percent after three years of service.
