Match formula, as filed

The Company's safe harbor matching contribution is 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation up to the amount that a participant contributes to the Plan and the specific match specified by individual collectively bargained agreements.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after one year of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after five years of credited service.
