Match formula, as filed

For eligible employees who have attained the age of 21, the University makes matching contributions equal to 100% of each participant’s elective deferrals up to 5% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Effective July 1, 2022, participants are 100% vested in the University’s contributions and earnings thereon upon completion of one year of service, as defined.
