Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Sponsor also makes a non-elective maximum contribution of 5% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are vested immediately in their contributions, as well as the Sponsor’s matching contribution portion of their accounts plus actual earnings thereon.
