Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a safe harbor matching contribution for eligible employees at a rate of 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of the eligible compensation contributed to the Plan, subject to certain limitations for employees of a certain participating employer.

Vesting, as filed
