Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions equal to 100% of elective deferral contributions which are not over 1% of base compensation, plus 50% of elective contributions which are over 1% but are not over 6% of the base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

For all other Company contributions, a participant is 20% vested after 2 years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after 6 years of credited service.
