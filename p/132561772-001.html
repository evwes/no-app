Match formula, as filed

Employer contributions match 100% on the first 1% of participants’ eligible earnings that are contributed on a pre-tax or Roth (after-tax) basis and 50% on the next 5% of eligible earnings that the employee contributes on a pre-tax or Roth (after-tax) basis.

Vesting, as filed

Participants fully vest in their Employer matching contribution and related earnings using a two-year cliff vesting schedule and are fully vested in their matching contributions when they reach their two-year anniversary with the Employer.
