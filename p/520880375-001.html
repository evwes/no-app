Match formula, as filed

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 8% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Institute contributes a non-elective Employer contribution equal to 8% of each employee’s eligible compensation up to the Social Security High Bend Point (SSHBP) and 10% of each employee’s eligible compensation above the SSHBP, as defined in the Plan document.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their voluntary contributions, rollover contributions, and Employer contributions, plus actual earnings and losses thereon.
