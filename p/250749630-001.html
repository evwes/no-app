Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company's nonelective contribution is based on employee classification and ranges from 5% to 14% of eligible compensation for participants that have completed one year of credited service, as defined.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are vested immediately in their contributions and the Company's nonelective contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
