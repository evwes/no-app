Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for Company Safe Harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of the participant's first 1% of compensation and 50% of the participant's next 5% of compensation immediately after completing twelve months of service.

Vesting, as filed

Employees vest in the Company's Safe Harbor matching contributions and actual earnings immediately and in any additional Company contributions after completion of two years of service, at which time they are fully vested.
