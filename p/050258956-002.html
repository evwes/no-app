Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 10% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Faculty Association members who are eligible participants of the Plan receive non-elective contributions in the amount of 10% of their compensation up to the Social Security taxable wage base and 15% of compensation in excess of the Social Security taxable wage base.
