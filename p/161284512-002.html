Match formula, as filed

The Company is required to make a matching contribution equal to 100% of each employee’s elective deferrals up to 3% of his or her compensation, as defined by the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Each plan year, the Company will make a nonelective contribution equal to 2% of the compensation of all participants eligible to share in allocations.

Vesting, as filed

Active participants as of March 1, 2020 are 100% vested in employer matching contributions.
