Match formula, as filed

Employer match – The Company matches 100% of employee contributions, up to the first 3% and then 50% of every dollar between 4% and 6% of eligible compensation deferred to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting – Participants are vested immediately in their contributions and Company matching contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
