Match formula, as filed

The Company’s matching contribution is 100% of the first 4% of eligible base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also makes non-elective safe harbor contributions equal to 4% of each participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed
