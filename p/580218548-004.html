Match formula, as filed

In addition, the Company makes matching contributions equal to one dollar for every dollar contributed by the participant up to 6% of the participant's eligible earnings, except however, that no matching contributions are made on catch-up contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Company Contributions - The Company makes nonelective contributions equal to 3% of eligible earnings for each participant.

Vesting, as filed

Company contributions and the related earnings and losses become 100% vested after two years of service, or upon the participant’s death, attainment of normal retirement age, or disability.
