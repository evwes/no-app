Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a discretionary match of 50% of the first 6% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan on a pay period basis, and makes an annual true-up contribution based on annual compensation, subsequent to period end.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 50% vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 50% until 100% vested after three years of credited service.
