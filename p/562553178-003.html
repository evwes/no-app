Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Kaiser Alum Inv Co Hourly Sav And Inv Plan, Kaiser Alum Inv Co Savings And Inv Plan, And The Kaiser Alum Warrick, Llc Sav And Inv Plan Mt. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 72.2% of the trust's $524 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
