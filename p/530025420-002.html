Match formula, as filed

The Plan calls for the Employer to make a matching contribution of 100% of the first 3% of employee’s pre-tax or Roth deferred compensation and 50% of the next 2% of employee’s pre-tax or Roth deferred compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants in the RSA will be 100% vested after three years of vesting service and will be eligible to take a distribution of vested balances upon termination of employment.
