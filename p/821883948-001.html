Match formula, as filed

The Company makes safe harbor matching contributions on employee deferrals up to 100% of the first 6% of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 9% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company made bi-weekly discretionary nonelective matching contributions equal to 9% of eligible compensation to eligible physicians and other credentialed providers who transitioned employment from the University of Oklahoma.

Vesting, as filed

In general, a participant is fully vested after five years of continuous service, although certain company contribution sources that were transferred into the Plan in February 2018, July 2021, January 2022, or March 2022 are subject to shorter vesting periods.
