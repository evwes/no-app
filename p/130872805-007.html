Match formula, as filed

Company Matching Contributions—The Company matches in cash all participant contributions at 70% on the first 4% of compensation contributed to the Plan and 50% on the next 4% of compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become 100% vested in Company matching contributions and RSA contributions, plus earnings thereon, after three years of service.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in International Paper Company Combined Defined Contribution Trust Fund - Retirement Income Fund. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 100.0% of the trust's $213 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
