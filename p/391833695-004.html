Match formula, as filed

Eligible participants under the Plan receive an employer matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 1% and 50% of the next 4% of a participant’s contributions, for a maximum possible matching contribution of 3% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Employer contributions vest 100% after two years of employment with the Company.
