Match formula, as filed

If a participant makes a contribution of pretax or after-tax compensation to the 401(k) component, the Company makes a matching contribution to the ESOP component equal to 50% of the first 6% of the compensation contributed by each participant.

Vesting, as filed

After three years of service, participants are 100% vested in their allocation of shares of the Company’s common stock held within the ESOP component, including amounts resulting from the Company matching contributions and discretionary contributions.
