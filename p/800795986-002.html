Match formula, as filed

The Company’s matching contributions are made at a rate of 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation contributed by a participant and 50% on the next 3% of eligible compensation to a maximum employer matching contribution of 4.5% of the employee’s contributed eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants may also request a distribution of their elective contributions and vested amounts of TransRe’s matching contributions while still employed by TransRe if they are at least 59½ years old or upon a proven hardship, as defined by the Plan.
