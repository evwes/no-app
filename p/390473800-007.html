Match formula, as filed

For support staff participating in the 401(k) Plan Account, the Firm matches 25% of the first 4% of participant contributions made during the plan year, not to exceed 1% of eligible compensation for eligible participants that are employed as of the last day of the Plan's year end.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service, or if the participant's service date is on or after attainment of the normal retirement age or as a result of death.
