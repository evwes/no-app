Match formula, as filed

Employer Contributions - The Company will make matching contributions to the Member’s account equal to 50% of a Member’s pre-tax contributions, Roth contributions and after-tax contributions, up to a maximum of 3% of a Member’s Salary.

Vesting, as filed
