Match formula, as filed

Participants receive a Company matching contribution equal to 75% of the first 6% ofa participant's contributions.

Vesting, as filed

If a participant was hired on or after March 1, 2017, the Company matching contributions made to the participant's account, and the earnings on these contributions, become vested after the participant has completed a three-year period of service.
