Match formula, as filed

Employer safe harbor matching – For MPAT and Local 396 union participants as well as those Local 1245 union employees hired on or after July 1, 2007, the Company matching contribution is 100% of the participant contribution, not to exceed 6% of the participant’s gross wages as defined in the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

MPAT, Local 396, and Local 1245 participants whose employment date or reemployment date is on or after January 1, 2017, are 100% vested in their fixed Company contributions plus actual earnings thereon after completion of three years of vesting service.
