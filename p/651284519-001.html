Match formula, as filed

The Company may make two separate discretionary contributions; a discretionary matching contribution equal to a uniform percentage of participant deferrals and a discretionary profit sharing contribution equal to a discretionary percentage of participant compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

As such, the Company provided for a fully vested qualified, nonelective contribution of 3% of compensation for all eligible participants totaling $3,010,356 and $3,090,698 during 2025 and 2024, respectively.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested in the discretionary contributions after three years of credited service, as defined in the plan document and 0% vested with less than three years of credited service.
