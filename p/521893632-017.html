Match formula, as filed

The Corporation contributes a matching contribution equal to 50% of the participant’s contribution up to the first 8% (i.e., up to 4%) of the participant’s base salary.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in all employee contributions, rollover contributions from other qualified plans, the Corporation's matching contributions and earnings (or losses) thereon.
