Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 100% of the first 4% of tax deferred compensation contributed to the Plan by all eligible employees.

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, the Company also makes a non-elective contribution for all eligible employees equal to 2% of eligible compensation.
