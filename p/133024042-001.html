Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes matching contributions of 50% of the amounts contributed by each participant, excluding rollovers, up to 6% of the participant’s eligible compensation, resulting in a maximum employer matching contribution of 3% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 100% vested in employer matching, employer non-elective, and profit sharing contributions, if applicable, after three years of credited service.
