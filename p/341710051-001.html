Match formula, as filed

The Company has a discretionary match of 100% of the participant contributions up to 3% of their annual compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3.3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company may also make a Qualified Automatic Contribution Arrangement (“QACA”) Safe Harbor (“SH”) non-elective contribution to Company employees equal to 3.3% of their annual compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become vested in discretionary match and related losses or earnings at a rate of 20% per year after one year of service, becoming 100% vested after five years of service.
