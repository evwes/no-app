Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company contributes 3% of a participant’s eligible pay for employees who are not actively participating in the Company’s Defined Benefit Plan, whether or not the employee contributes.

Vesting, as filed

All employee and Employer contributions are 100% vested immediately.
