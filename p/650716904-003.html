Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions equal to 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of a participant’s eligible compensation that is contributed as an elective deferral.

Vesting, as filed

Participants that become eligible after April 12, 2010 vest in the Company contributions portion of their accounts, plus actual earnings thereon, at 20% per Year of Service up to 100% at five (5) or more years of service.
