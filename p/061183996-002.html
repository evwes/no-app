Match formula, as filed

During 2025, the Company made discretionary matching contributions of 100% of the first 5% of eligible compensation that was contributed on a bi-weekly basis by each participant.

Vesting, as filed

The participants’ rights to the matching amounts contributed by the Company, plus earnings thereon, vest at a rate Argo Group US 401(k) (continued) December 31, 2025 of 20% for year one, 40% for year two, and 60% for year three and are fully vested after three years of service.
