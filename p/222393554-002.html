Match formula, as filed

Eligible nonunion employees receive safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100 percent of the first 3 percent of the participant's eligible contribution and 50 percent of the next 4 percent of the participant's eligible contribution.

Vesting, as filed

Participants vest in the Company's safe harbor matching contribution at 50 percent after one year of service and are fully vested after two years of service.
