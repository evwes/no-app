Match formula, as filed

ServCo will make matching contributions ("Matching Contributions") equal to 50% of the Elective Contributions and/or after-tax contributions made by each Participant, which do not exceed the first 8% of the Participant's annual eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Matching and Core Contributions to a Participant's account are vested upon a Participant's completion of three years of service, or when a Participant reaches the age of 65, is disabled or dies.
