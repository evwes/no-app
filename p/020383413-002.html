Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2024, participants of Freudenberg Performance Materials LP receive a fixed matching contribution of 100% of the first 2% and 50% of the next 4% of the participant’s elective deferrals.

Vesting, as filed

Unless otherwise noted below, vesting in the Employers’ matching contribution portions of a participant’s account is based on a two-year cliff and the Employers’ non-elective contribution portions of a participant’s account is based on a three-year cliff.
