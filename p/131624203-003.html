Match formula, as filed

The Plan includes an Employer matching contribution on 100% of the first 3% of base salary that an employee contributes into the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are fully vested in the Employer matching contributions upon the earlier of (a) the time when the employee completes three years of service, (b) the date the employee attains age 65, or (c) the date of the employee’s death.
