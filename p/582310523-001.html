Match formula, as filed

The Plan has a Safe Harbor matching provision, which requires the Company to match, at a minimum, 100% of the first 4% of covered compensation, as defined in the plan agreement, and 50% of the next 1% of covered compensation, for a maximum match of 4.5% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting - Participants are immediately 100% vested in all elective deferrals, employer Safe Harbor matching contributions and employer discretionary contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
