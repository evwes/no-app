Match formula, as filed

The Corporation's contribution is based on matching 100% of the first 4% of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Plan also provides for nonelective contributions equal to 1% of the participant's compensation for the plan year.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their contributions and the Corporation's matching contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
