Match formula, as filed

The Company made employer discretionary matching contributions in 2025 and 2024 equal to 50% of the employee elective deferrals up to 6% of eligible compensation contributed to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3.41% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company made discretionary nonelective contributions of 3.41% and 3.53% of eligible compensation for each eligible employee that was employed at December 31, 2025 and December 31, 2024, respectively.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting - Participants are fully vested at all times in that portion of their accounts attributable to their own contributions and to Company discretionary matching contributions.
