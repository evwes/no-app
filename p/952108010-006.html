Match formula, as filed

The Company will make an IRC Safe Harbor Matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 1%, 60% of the next 4% and then 10% of the next 1% of eligible compensation deferred by each participant.

Vesting, as filed

A participant becomes 100% vested in the Company matching contributions after one year of continuous service.
