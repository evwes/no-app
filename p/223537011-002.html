Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2025, the Company elected to make a discretionary matching contribution of 25% of the first 6% of eligible deferral contributions made by each participant.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

For the year ended December 31, 2025, the Company made non-elective safe harbor contributions of 3% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant vests 20% per year starting with two years of service and is 100% vested after six years of credited service.
