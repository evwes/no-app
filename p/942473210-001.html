Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 30% of each participant’s deferrals each payroll period, excluding catch-up and after-tax contributions, up to 15% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired on or prior to January 1, 2021 are 100% vested in the Company’s matching and true-up matching contributions plus earnings thereon; participants hired after January 1, 2021 vest 100% after three years of credited service.
