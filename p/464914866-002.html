Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for employer matching contributions (made on a pre-tax basis) equal to 100% of the first 5% of a Participant’s eligible compensation deferred as pre-tax and/or Roth contributions, determined on a per-pay-period basis.

Vesting, as filed
