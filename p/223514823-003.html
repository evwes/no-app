Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a matching contribution to the Plan of an amount equal to 100% of the first 6% of salary that the employee has elected to contribute.

Vesting, as filed

Company contributions are 100% vested after three years of service.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Ingredion Incorporated Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 88.4% of the trust's $831 million; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
