Match formula, as filed

The Corporation makes matching contributions equal to 100% of elective deferrals, up to (1) 6% of each participant's Plan year compensation if hired on or after January 1, 2011; or (2) 8% of each participant's Plan year compensation if hired before January 1, 2011.
