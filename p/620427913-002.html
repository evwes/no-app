Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes a basic matching contribution of 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Under the non-contributory provision, the Employer contributes an amount equal to 3.5% of the participant’s eligible compensation into the participant’s account up to a maximum eligible compensation limit determined by the IRS.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Under the contributory provision, participants are immediately 100% vested in both the participant’s and Employer’s matching contributions.
