Match formula, as filed

The Company provides a matching contribution to all employees eligible to participate in the Plan equal to 50% of each participant’s elective deferrals up to the first 6% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 50% vested in employer contributions after one year of vesting service and are fully vested upon the completion of two years of service.
