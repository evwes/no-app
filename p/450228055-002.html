Match formula, as filed

The Society will make matching contributions in an amount equal to 100% of a participant’s elective deferral contributions that are not over 4% of compensation, plus 50% of elective deferral contributions that are over 4% but not over 6% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after two years of credited service.
