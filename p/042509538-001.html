Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes 3% of base compensation as a safe harbor contribution and matches 100% of the first 3% of base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan for all participants with a minimum of 1,000 hours of service in the 12-month period after becoming an employee.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.
