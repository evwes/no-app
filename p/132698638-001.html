Match formula, as filed

Automatically enrolled participants have their deferral rate set at 5% of eligible compensation, subject to annual automatic increases of 1% up to 10% of eligible compensation, and their contributions invested in a designated balanced fund until changed by the participant.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also contributes a non-elective safe harbor contribution annually in the amount of 3% of participants' base wages.

Vesting, as filed

A participant vests 20% after one year of credited service and 20% for each year of credited service thereafter, becoming 100% vested after five years of credited service.
