Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions to the Plan equal to 100% of each participant’s contributions, up to the first 3% of their eligible compensation, and 50% of each participant’s contributions, up to the next 2% of their eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are fully vested in their employer match and retirement contribution accounts after the earlier of three years of vesting service or upon attaining age 65.
