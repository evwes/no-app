Match formula, as filed

Oracle matches 50% of an active participant’s salary deferrals up to a maximum deferral of 6% of compensation for the pay period, with maximum aggregate matching of $5,100 in any calendar year.

Vesting, as filed

Participants’ vesting in Oracle’s matching contributions is based on years of service.
