Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 100 percent of participant contributions up to 5.0 percent of base salary and any recognized sales and performance-based incentive compensation for employee contributions into the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also makes non-elective contributions of 4.5 percent of earnings for all eligible employees.

Vesting, as filed

- Continued Unum Group 401(k) Retirement Plan December 31, 2025 Note 1 - Description of the Plan - Continued Vesting The Company and participant contributions, plus actual earnings and market value adjustments thereon, are immediately 100 percent vested at the date of contribution.
