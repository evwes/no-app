Match formula, as filed

All other union employees are eligible for an employer matching contribution up to 25% of salary deferral contributions, not to exceed 4% of compensation and additionally are eligible for a discretionary employer match of up to 1% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 100% vested in their employer matching contribution and discretionary contribution after five years of credited service (20% per year after one year of credited service).
