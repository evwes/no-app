Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 5% of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

An additional non-elective profit-sharing contribution equal to 5% of annual compensation is contributed by the Company.
