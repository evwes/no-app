Match formula, as filed

The Employers contribute an amount into a participant’s Matching Account equal to 100% of the first 1% of the amount of pre-tax and/or Roth contributions and 50% of the pre-tax and/or Roth contributions from 2% to 6% of compensation (up to 3.5% of eligible earnings).

Vesting, as filed

Prior to January 1 2024, Participants were 100% vested in matching contributions after two years of continuous employment or immediately upon death, disability or attaining age 65, while employed by the Company.
