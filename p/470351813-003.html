Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 75% of the first 6% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan unless the participant is covered by a collective bargaining agreement, in which case the agreement’s terms apply.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in the Company’s matching contributions and related earnings occurs at a rate of 25% per year of service, with full vesting after four years.
