Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2025, the Company approved a discretionary matching contribution to the Plan based on a calculation of the lesser of 50% of the first 9% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan or $8,500.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after two years of credited service, 40% vested after three years of credited service, 60% vested after four years of credited service, and 100% vested after five years of credited service.
