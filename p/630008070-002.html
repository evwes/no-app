Match formula, as filed

For participants employed by the Company, the basic employer matching contribution is equal to 100% of participant elective deferrals on the first 3% of base compensation, plus 50% of elective deferrals on the next 3% of base compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Nonelective contributions for participants employed by the Company range from 2% to 8% of compensation based on the sum of a participants age and years of service.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become 100% vested in QACA safe harbor matching contributions after two years of credited service and 100% vested in basic matching and nonelective contributions, including transition contributions, after five years of credited service.
