Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2024, the Company matched 50% of the first 6% of employee contributions and additionally provided employees with a discretionary cash contribution up to 3% of their total quarterly pay.

Vesting, as filed

Employer contributions vest at a rate of 40% after completion of one year and thereafter at 20% per year of service until fully vested after four years.
