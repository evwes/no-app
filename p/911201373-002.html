Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of pre-tax and/or Roth contributions up to 4% of eligible earnings to all employees who make contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company contributes a nonelective contribution of 2% or 4% of eligible earnings (depending on years of service) to certain collectively bargained employees per the terms of their collective bargaining agreement.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after five years of service.
