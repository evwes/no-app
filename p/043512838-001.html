Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2024, the Company approved a discretionary matching contribution equal to 100% of a participant's contributions up to 6% of their eligible compensation, up to a maximum of $15,300 per participant per year.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 33% vested after one year of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 34% and 33%, respectively, until 100% vested after three years of credited service.
