Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 100% of employees’ pre-tax contributions, up to 4% of each employee’s eligible compensation after one year of employment.

Vesting, as filed

Profit Sharing Thrift Plan (the TOCA Plan) with profit-sharing contributions attributable to the TOCA Plan vest in those contributions based on credited service, 20% vested after their second year of service and an additional 20% each year until fully vested after six years of service.
