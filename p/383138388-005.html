Match formula, as filed

Participants in the Plan prior to April 16, 2018 are eligible to receive a Company matching contribution equal to 50%, up to the first 10% of participant contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Participants also receive an additional non-elective Company contribution between 3% and 5% of eligible salary, based on years of service.
