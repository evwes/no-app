Match formula, as filed

For December 31, 2025 and 2024, management elected to make a matching contribution equal to 50 percent of the first 6 percent of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan for a maximum equal to 3 percent of compensation, or $1,200 if a “special class” employee as defined by the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

An additional enhanced benefit non-elective contribution of 4 percent of compensation is made to participants employed at year end that are not eligible to participate in the Group Retirement Income Plan No.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 100 percent vested in the enhanced benefit employer matching contributions and the enhanced benefit employer non-elective matching contributions after three years of service or upon attaining normal retirement age.
