Match formula, as filed

Noblis will make a dollar-for-dollar matching contribution equal to the E-2 Contributions made by the participant for the same payroll period, not to exceed 6% of the participant’s base salary for that payroll period (N-2 Contributions) for those employees hired prior to January 1, 2020.

Vesting, as filed

Participants who were hired on or after January 1, 2010, and were actively employed with the Company on January 1, 2015, or were rehired or hired by the Company after January 1, 2015, are 100% vested in all contributions and related earnings to their account.
