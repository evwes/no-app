Match formula, as filed

The Basic Employer Matching Contribution is generally 100% on the first 1% and 50% on the next 5% of pre-tax deferrals or Roth 401(k) contributions to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition to the Basic Employer Matching Contribution, Participating Employers make an annual automatic non-elective contribution equal to the greater of 1% of pay or 750 dollars to employees who are actively employed on the last day of the Plan Year.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting All participants are fully vested immediately with respect to their own pre-tax deferrals, Roth 401(k) contributions, catch-up contributions and Basic Employer Matching Contributions.
