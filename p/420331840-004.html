Match formula, as filed

Participants of certain companies are automatically enrolled with an initial 4% to 6% salary deferral contribution and their salary deferral contribution will increase by 1% up to 10% (15% effective April 2026) on the month following the participant’s anniversary date.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Certain participating companies make non-elective contributions at a range of 2% to 20% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Employer matching, non-elective, and discretionary non-elective vesting schedules vary by Company and range from immediately vested to fully vested after four years of service (six years of service prior to January 1, 2025).
