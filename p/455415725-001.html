Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for the matching of employee contributions with an amount equal to 100% of such contributions up to 2% of the eligible employees’ compensation plus 50% of such contributions on the next 4% of the eligible employees’ compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are not vested in Company contributions until completion of 2 years of service with the Company, at which time they become 100% vested in Company contributions.
