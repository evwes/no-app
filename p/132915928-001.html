Match formula, as filed

These contributions are recorded in a “company matching account.” Effective July 1, 2017, all non-union employees receive company matching contributions of 100% of the first 5% of eligible earnings.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company provides non-elective contributions of 4% of compensation for all participants after one year of service, which is subject to a three-year vesting requirement.

Vesting, as filed

Company matching contributions are vested 100% immediately.
