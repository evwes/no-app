Match formula, as filed

Prior to June 1, 2025, the Participating Employers contributed a matching contribution equal to 50% of participant contributions up to 8% of eligible compensation, as deﬁned by the Plan, for participants in Program B.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Participating Employers make a non-elective contribution equal to 5 percent of each eligible Program B participant's eligible compensation, as deﬁned by the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants of both Programs become fully vested in all other Participating Employers' contributions after completing three years of credited service.
