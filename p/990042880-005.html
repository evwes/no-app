Match formula, as filed

After one year of service, the Company provides a matching contribution equal to 2% of the participant’s eligible earnings up to 100% of the employee’s pre-tax and Roth deferrals for participants with 20 years or less of service with the Company.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also provides a nonelective contribution of 5% of eligible earnings for participants who have completed one or more years of service with the Company.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting - Participants are vested immediately in all contributions.
