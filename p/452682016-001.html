Match formula, as filed

The Company matches participant contributions (“Employer Safe Harbor Contributions”) at a rate of 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation, and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation, as detailed in the plan document.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 100% vested in the Employer Contributions portion of their accounts after two years of continuous credited service, with the exception of Employer Safe Harbor Contributions, which participants are 100% vested when the contributions are made.
