Match formula, as filed

Employer matching contributions are equal to 100% of the first 3% of participants' compensation deferred to the Plan and 50% of the next 3% of participants’ compensation deferred to the Plan and totaled $8,632,870 for 2024.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after one year of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after five years of credited service.
