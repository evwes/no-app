Match formula, as filed

The System will make a matching contribution in an amount equal to 50% of the first 4% of pay that is deferred to the Plan as long as the participant is employed on the last day of the plan year and has completed one year of service and at least 1,000 hours.

Vesting, as filed

In all other situations, the System contributions are 100% vested upon completion of three years of credited service.
