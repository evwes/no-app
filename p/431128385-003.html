Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides a plan sponsor safe harbor match of 100% of participant contributions up to a maximum of 5% of the participant’s annual eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in the Company non-elective contribution is based on years of service with an employee vesting 20% after two years of service and subsequently vesting 20% each year until becoming fully vested with six years of continuous service.
