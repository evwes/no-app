Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 7.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Effective January 1, 2023, the approved nonelective contribution was equal to 7.5% for eligible employees with two years of service or more and 5% for eligible employees with more than one year of service but less than two years of service.
