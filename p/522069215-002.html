Match formula, as filed

The Company matches 100% of the first 2% plus 50% of the next 4% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company makes an additional non-elective contribution equal to 3% of eligible compensation or a minimum contribution of $2,000 to the Plan for all eligible employees, subject to the Plan’s eligibility requirements.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested in the Company’s matching contribution and the non-elective 3% contribution after two years of service.
