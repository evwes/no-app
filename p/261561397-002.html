Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2021, the Plan constitutes a safe harbor plan and provides for a matching employer contribution equal to 100% of the first 5% of pre-tax compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan as Pre-Tax deferral contributions and/or Roth elective deferrals.

Vesting, as filed

Effective January 1, 2020, employer’s matching contributions become 100% vested after two years of service.
