Match formula, as filed

The Company provides an employer matching contribution of 50% of the participant's elective deferrals, not to exceed 6% of the participant's compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their contributions plus actual earnings thereon and are vested in the employer match and Company discretionary non-elective profit sharing contribution after (a) three years of credited service, (b) total disability, or (c) death.
