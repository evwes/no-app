Match formula, as filed

The Company is required to make safe harbor matching contributions equal to 100% of the first 2% of a participant’s salary deferral contributions plus 50% of the next 4% of a participant’s salary deferral contributions calculated on a pay period basis.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company is also required to make a non-elective contribution to all eligible employees equal to 3% of eligible compensation calculated on a pay period basis.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become fully vested in the Company’s non-election contributions after three years of service.
