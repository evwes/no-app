Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for Safe Harbor Employer Matching contributions in an amount equal to 100% of the first 3% of the participant's compensation contributed plus 50% of the amount of the deposits that exceed 3% of the participant's compensation but do not exceed 5% of the participant's compensation.

Vesting, as filed
