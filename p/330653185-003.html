Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for Safe Harbor Employer Matching contributions in an amount equal to 100% of the first 3% of the participant's compensation contributed plus 50% of the amount of the deposits that exceed 3% of the participant's compensation but do not exceed 5% of the participant's compensation.

Vesting, as filed

The Company's discretionary matching and profit sharing contributions for participants terminated prior to January 1, 2020 are subject to the previous three-year cliff vesting schedule.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Leidos, Inc. Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 73.1% of the trust's $12.6 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
