Match formula, as filed

The Company matches contributions made by employees into the Plan at an amount equal to 100% of a participant's eligible elective deferrals up to 3% of the participant's eligible compensation and 50% of the next 4% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

on or after January 1, 2022 vest in their allocated share of employer matching contributions at 0% for less than two years of service and 100% for two or more years of service.
