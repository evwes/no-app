Match formula, as filed

The Organization made matching contributions in the amount of 50% of the first 8% of participant's elective deferral based on the participants completing one year of service and attaining the age of 21.

Vesting, as filed

For participants who terminated on or after October 1, 2015, the Organization’s discretionary matching contribution and discretionary profit sharing contribution and related earnings vest at 50% after two years of service and 100% after three years of service.
