Match formula, as filed

For non-union employees, the Company provides matching contributions of i) 100% of each participant’s contribution for the first 1% of such participant’s eligible compensation and ii) 50% of each participant’s contribution for the next 5% of such participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

For non-union employees, Company contributions plus actual earnings thereon contributed are 100% vested following two years of service.
