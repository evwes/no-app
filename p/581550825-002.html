Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for a safe harbor matching contribution of 100% on the first 3% of the participant’s compensation, plus 50% on the next 3% of the participants compensation, as defined in the Plan document.

Vesting, as filed

Participants who fail to complete an hour of service on or after January 1, 2019 vest 100% in the Company’s matching contribution after three years of service, or upon the occurrence of their 65th birthday, total or permanent disability, or death.
