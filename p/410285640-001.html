Match formula, as filed

The Company matched 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation for the year ended December 31, 2025.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, eligible employees hired or rehired on or after January 1, 2006 receive a company basic contribution of 2.0% of eligible compensation in lieu of participating in a Company-sponsored defined-benefit plan.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are vested after 60 days of employment in employee and company contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
