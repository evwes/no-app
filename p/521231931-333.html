Match formula, as filed

The Organization contributes a non-discretionary matching contribution equal to $.50 for each $1.00 by which a participant defers their compensation up to the first 6% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Employer matching and discretionary contributions vest based on years of vesting service as follows: 0% vested for less than one year of vesting service, 33% vested after one year of vesting service, 66% vested after two years of vesting service, and 100% vested after three years of vesting service.
