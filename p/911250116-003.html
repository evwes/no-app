Match formula, as filed

The Organization makes matching contributions equal to 35% of the first 6% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested in employer matching and discretionary contributions and earnings thereon after three years of credited service with no partial vesting prior to three years of vested service.
