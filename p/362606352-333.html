Match formula, as filed

The Company may make a discretionary matching contribution to the Plan equal to 50 percent of an employee's contribution, up to 6 percent of annual gross compensation deferred.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, annual nonelective contributions to the Plan are made by the Company in an amount equal to 3 percent of each participant's annual gross compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100 percent vested after three years of credited service.
