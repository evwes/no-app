Match formula, as filed

…The Plan includes a matching contribution feature for Plan participants hired before October 1, 2010, which provides that the Company will contribute $0.25 for every $1.00 of salary reduction contributions by a participant to their 401(k) account up to a maximum of 4% of their eligible wages.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 6.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Effective January 1, 2024, the Plan was amended and the nonelective core contribution was increased to 6.5% for the 2024 plan year, 7% for the 2025 plan year, and 7.5% for the 2026 plan year and subsequent years for eligible participants hired on or after October 1, 2010.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
