Match formula, as filed

The Plan allows for a matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 6% of the participant’s eligible compensation that the employee contributes as elective deferrals.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company makes a nonelective profit sharing contribution on behalf of each eligible employee equal to 3% of such eligible employee’s eligible compensation per pay period.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become vested in the Company contributions, and the earnings on those amounts, at 20% for each year of credited vesting service, with participants fully vested in those contributions after five years of credited vesting service.
