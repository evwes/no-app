Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2024 and the period January 1, 2025 through September 30, 2025, the Employer matching contribution was 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation that a participant elects to defer into the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

From October 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025 the Employer made a discretionary nonelective contribution equal to 4% of eligible compensation totaling approximately $1,173,000.
