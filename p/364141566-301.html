Match formula, as filed

The Company makes matching contributions to participants' accounts based on their employee contributions in an amount equal to 100% of the first 6% of their compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Schneider Electric 401(k) Vesting All actively employed participants as of or after January 1, 2010, are immediately vested in their own contributions, Company matching contributions, and any of the Company's nonelective contributions, plus actual earnings thereon.
