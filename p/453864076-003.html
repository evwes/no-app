Match formula, as filed

The Plan includes multiple employers of the Company who, depending on the employer, make matching contributions ranging from 50-100% on the first 4% of base compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan, up to a maximum of 2% or 4% of annual compensation.

Vesting, as filed

In the event of Plan termination, participants would become 100% vested in the employer contribution portion of their accounts.
