Match formula, as filed

The amount of the matching contributions equals 50% of the first 6% of the respective participant’s eligible compensation that the participant contributes to the Plan in the form of pretax deferrals, subject to Internal Revenue Code limits.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Charity makes nonelective contributions in an amount equal to 3% of a participant’s eligible compensation up to the social security taxable wage base, plus 6% of the participant’s eligible compensation in excess of the social security taxable wage base.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become fully vested in the employer matching and employer non-elective contributions and related earnings at the completion of two years of service, as defined in the Plan document.
