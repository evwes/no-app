Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2025 and 2024 participants who are eligible employees of PHH, DuBois, Elk, Brookville, J.C Blair, and Tyrone Hospital received a matching contribution of 100% of employees’ salary deferral amounts on up to 4% of employees eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

All participants will be fully vested in their employer matching contributions (plus earnings thereon) after completing three years of service.
