Match formula, as filed

In addition, the Hospital made a match of 50% of any participant's deferral amount up to 2% of that participant's base pay if the participant's age plus credited years of service with the Hospital as of December 31, 2011, was a value equaling 70 or higher.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after six years of credited service.
