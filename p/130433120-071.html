Match formula, as filed

Employees not eligible for pension coverage receive an enhanced matching contribution from the Corporation of 50 percent of the first 6 percent of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan, up to a maximum of 3 percent.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting – The Corporation’s contributions allocated to each participant will become 100 percent vested after three years of vesting service, attainment of normal retirement age, total and permanent disability, or death.
