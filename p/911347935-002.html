Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a matching contribution equal to 100% of participants’ salary deferrals up to 4% of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also makes a nonelective retirement contribution equal to 4% of eligible compensation to participants employed by the Company on the last day of the Plan year.

Vesting, as filed

Nonelective retirement contributions are subject to a three year cliff vesting schedule.
