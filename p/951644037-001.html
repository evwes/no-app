Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for discretionary University matching contributions, and for the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024, the University matched each participant’s deferral at the rate of 100 percent of the first six percent of the participant’s compensation deferred.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting - Participants are vested immediately in their contributions and University matching and nonelective contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
