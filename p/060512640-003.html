Match formula, as filed

The Firm contributes matching contributions equal to 100% of each participant’s first 1% of salary deferral contributions or Roth contributions and an additional 50% of the next 5% in a plan year. Par

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 6.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Firm also makes nonelective contributions on periodic basis equal to 6.5% of the participant’s eligible compensation, as defined by the Plan, plus an additional amount equal to 5.4% of the participant’s excess compensation.
