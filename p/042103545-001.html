Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 8% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The University contributes 8% of eligible compensation for participants with fewer than nine years of service and 10% of eligible compensation for participants with nine or more years of service.
