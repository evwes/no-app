Match formula, as filed

The Plan includes a safe-harbor provision which requires the Company to make matching contributions equal to 100% of participant elective deferrals that do not exceed 3% of compensation plus 50% of salary deferrals between 3% and 5% of compensation. Ad

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Additionally, the Plan provides for a Company non-elective contribution equal to 3% of compensation for active participants.

Vesting, as filed

A participant becomes 100% vested after two years of credited service for employer safe harbor matching contributions and three years of credited service for employer non-elective contributions.
