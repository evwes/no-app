Match formula, as filed

A Plan participant receives an employer-matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 2% of the participant’s contribution and 50% of the next 4% of the participant’s contribution for a total 4% employer-matching contribution on a deferral of 6% of a participant’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Employer-matching contributions made after April 1, 2008, are subject to a two-year cliff vesting upon completing two years of credited service.
