Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a non-discretionary match of 50 percent of the first 6 percent of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 4% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company also makes a safe harbor non-elective contribution for eligible employees equal to 4 percent of the employee's eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100 percent vested after 2 years of credited service.
