Match formula, as filed

Participants are also eligible for an employer matching contribution of 100 percent of participant 401(k) contributions, up to a maximum of 6 percent of eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3%–5% of pay (rate varies per the filing) regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Based upon the participant's point total, a non-elective contribution of 3, 4 or 5 percent of eligible compensation will be made after the close of the Plan year.

Vesting, as filed

Participants fully vest in the employer non-elective contributions and the related earnings thereon after being credited with three years of vesting service.
