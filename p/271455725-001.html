Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for a safe harbor matching contribution to each eligible participant's account in an amount equal to 100% of the first 3%, plus 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation, as defined, that a participant contributes to the Plan for the plan year.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 20% vested after one year of credited service and continue to vest in increments of 20% until becoming 100% vested after five years of credited service.
