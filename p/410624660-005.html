Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides a safe harbor matching contribution consisting of 100% of the first 3% of the participant’s eligible pay that is deferred on a pre-tax basis and 50% of the next 2% of eligible pay that is deferred on a pre-tax basis.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are immediately vested in the safe harbor matching contribution, plus earnings thereon.
