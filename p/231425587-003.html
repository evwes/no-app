Match formula, as filed

A participant credited with two years of service has a 20% vested interest in the Firm matching contributions and Firm discretionary contributions and earnings thereon.

Vesting, as filed

The vested interest increases at the rate of 20% for each year of service thereafter so that a participant with six years of service is 100% vested in Firm matching contributions and Firm discretionary contributions allocated to his/her accounts.
