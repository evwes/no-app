Match formula, as filed

Each pay period, Herc Rentals makes a qualified matching contribution to the Plan equal to 100% of the first 3% of the employee’s deferrals plus 50% of deferrals between 3% and 5%, not to exceed 4% of total employee compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Contributions made between January 1, 2002 and June 30, 2016, were subject to a cliff vesting schedule of three years.
