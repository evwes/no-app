Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the amount of salary deferrals that do not exceed 3% of the participant’s eligible compensation and 50% of the amount of salary deferrals that exceed 3% of compensation but do not exceed 5% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

These contributions and the gains and losses credited thereon, vested over a 6-year graded schedule with participants becoming 20% vested after 2 years of service and another 20% each year thereafter until 100% vested after six years.
