Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a QACA safe-harbor matching contribution of 100% of the first 1%; 75% of the next 1%; and 50% of the next 4% of participants' pretax contributions, determined each payroll period.

Vesting, as filed

For each participant credited with at least one hour of service on or after January 1, 2019, the QACA matching contributions account, non-QACA matching contributions account and retirement contributions account vest 50% after one year of service and 100% after two or more years of service.
