Match formula, as filed

The Plan Sponsor is required to make “Safe Harbor” matching contributions equal to 100% of the first 3% of compensation contributed to the Plan by eligible employees, and 50% of the next 2% of compensation contributed to the Plan by eligible employees each year.

Vesting, as filed

The Plan has adopted a three-year cliff vesting schedule in accordance with the Pension Protection Act of 2006.
