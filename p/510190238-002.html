Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for a dollar for dollar matching contribution up to 4% of a participant's eligible compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Plan also provides for a non-elective employer contribution equal to 2% of each participant's eligible compensation up to the Social Security Wage Base (SSWB), plus 5% of the amount above the SSWB but less than the IRS salary limit.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their contributions and any matching contributions plus actual earnings thereon.
