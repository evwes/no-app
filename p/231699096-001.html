Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for non-discretionary matching contributions to participants equal to $0.50 for each $1.00 contributed by a participant, up to a maximum of 6% of the participant’s compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in non-discretionary matching contributions and discretionary non-elective contributions portion of their accounts is based on years of continuous service and are subject to a 3-year cliff vesting schedule.
