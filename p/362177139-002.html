Match formula, as filed

Employees can elect to contribute 1% or 2% of their salary and the University will match 200% of employees’ voluntary contributions for a maximum University contribution of 8%, including the mandatory University contribution and the University match.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

7 THE NOTE 1 DESCRIPTION OF PLAN (continued) Contributions Prior to July 1, 2016, the University contributed 2.5% of a participant’s compensation (including salary, extra service pay, and overtime pay) and the participants contributed 3% of their compensation.

Vesting, as filed

University employees hired after that date are fully vested in their personal contributions and are 100% vested in the University’s contributions after three years of service.
