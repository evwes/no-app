Match formula, as filed

Under the amendment, each Participating Company will make matching contributions equal to 100% of a participant’s matched contributions for each pay period, up to the first 5% of the participant’s compensation, subject to applicable compensation limits.

Vesting, as filed

These contributions vest at rates of 20% per year of credited service and become 100% vested after five years of credited service.
