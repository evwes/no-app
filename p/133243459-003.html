Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes as a safe harbor employer matching contribution, 100% up to 5% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

During the year ended December 31, 2025, the non-elective discretionary contribution was 5% of eligible participants’ 401(k)-eligible earnings.

Vesting, as filed
