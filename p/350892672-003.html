Match formula, as filed

The Hospital matches 50% of employees’ salary deferral amounts on the first 4% of employees’ compensation.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2.3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

During 2025 and 2024, the discretionary nonelective contribution is 2.3% of a participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is fully vested after six years of continuous service.
