Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for a Company match of 100% of the first 2% of each participant’s voluntary contribution and 25% of the next 4% of each participant’s voluntary contribution, funded in conjunction with the employee contribution.

Vesting, as filed

Employer contributions, and earnings on employer contributions, vest based on years of service with the Company at a rate of 33% per year from years two through four and are 100% vested after four years of service.
