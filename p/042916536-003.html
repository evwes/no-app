Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for safe harbor matching contributions whereby the Company contributes 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 2% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Plan also provides for nondiscretionary nonelective contributions whereby the Company contributes 2% of a participant's eligible compensation, subject to statutory limitations.

Vesting, as filed

With regard to Company matching contributions, a participant is 25% vested after two years of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 25% until 100% vested after five years of credited service.
