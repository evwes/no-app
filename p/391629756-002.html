Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a non-discretionary match of 50 percent of the first 6 percent of a participant's pre-tax and/or Roth payroll contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

The Company contributes 3 percent of eligible pay to participants with less than 10 years of service and 4 percent of eligible pay to participants with 10 or more years of service.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 25 percent vested after 1 year of credited service and continues to vest in increments of 25 percent until 100 percent vested after 4 years of credited service.
