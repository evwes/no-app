Match formula, as filed

As a “Safe Harbor Plan,” the Company makes a fully vested safe harbor matching contribution for all eligible participants amounting to 100% of the first 3% of the participant’s salary deferred and 50% of the next 2% of salary deferred.

Vesting, as filed

Discretionary matching and profit sharing contributions vest 20% each year after one year of service, with full vesting after five years.
