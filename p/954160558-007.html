Match formula, as filed

Lyondell and the Participating Employers make safe harbor matching contributions on behalf of participants at the rate of 100% of the Elective Deferrals (excluding catch-up contributions) up to a maximum matching contribution of 6% of each participant’s eligible earnings.

Vesting, as filed

As of January 1, 2009, matching contributions and earnings thereon are immediately fully vested.
