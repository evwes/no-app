Match formula, as filed

The System contributes a safe harbor match of 100% of the participant’s elective deferrals that do not exceed 4% of eligible compensation, plus 50% of the participant’s elective deferrals that exceed 4% of eligible compensation but do not exceed 6% of eligible compensation for the allocation period.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after two years of credited service.
