Match formula, as filed

The Participant's Employer contributes an amount equal to 50% of each Participant's first 8% of eligible compensation made as Deferred Deposits and/or Nondeferred Deposits as its matching contribution to the Plan ("Employer Matching Contributions").

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Public Service Enterprise Group Inc. Master Defined Contribution Retirement Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 45.4% of the trust's $4.4 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
