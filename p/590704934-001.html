Match formula, as filed

The Clinic makes matching contributions equal to 50% of the first 4% of compensation and 100% of the next 2% of compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan up to a maximum of 4% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in Clinic discretionary contributions is based on years of continuous service, with 100% vesting after three years of credited service.
