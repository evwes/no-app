Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides eligible salaried employees, excluding former Ford Favesa employees, with a Company matching contribution of 100% of the first 3% of compensation contributed to the Plan, plus 50% of the next 3% of compensation contributed to the Plan, subject to certain limitations.
