Match formula, as filed

The Company also makes an employer matching contribution for eligible participants of the Company and TILLC at the rate of 50% of the first 4% of eligible pay, up to an annual maximum contribution of $3,500 and a Company discretionary contribution when decided.

Vesting, as filed

The Plan was amended in 2006 to change the vesting schedule of employer matching contributions and earnings thereon, based on years of credited service as follows: Years of Service Vesting (%) 1 25 2 50 3 100 A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
