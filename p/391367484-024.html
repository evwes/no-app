Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a Safe Harbor Matching Contribution consisting of 100% on the first 3% of participant contributions and 50% on the next 2% of participant contributions.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Certain eligible participants also receive an additional Age Weighted Nonelective Contribution between 1% and 9% of annual pay or a nonelective contribution equal to 2% of annual pay.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become 20% vested after completing two years of service and are additionally vested at 20% for each year of additional service up to six years of service, at which time participants are fully vested.
