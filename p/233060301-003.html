Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a matching contribution of up to 6% of the employee’s eligible compensation, with the exception of non-union TOTAL USVI participants (an eligible employee employed in connection with the Total USVI Division of Total Petroleum Puerto Rico Corp).

Vesting, as filed

Vesting in the non-contributory contribution accounts of eligible union employees is based on a three-year vesting schedule, with participants becoming 100% vested after three years of non-contributory service.
