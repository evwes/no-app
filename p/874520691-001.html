Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2024, each payroll period, the Company matches 100% of the first 6% of pretax and/or Roth 401(k) contributions participants make to the Plan, including any catch-up contributions.

Vesting, as filed

Participants will be become 100% vested in the company matching contributions made under the Plan on and after January 1, 2024 upon completing 3 years of service.
