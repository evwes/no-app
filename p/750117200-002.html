Match formula, as filed

For the year ended December 31, 2024, the Employer matched participant deferrals at the rate of $0.25 for each $1.00 contributed by the participant, up to 8% of compensation per pay period, with a maximum match ranging from $1,500 to $3,000, depending on the years of service.

Vesting, as filed

Vesting Participants are immediately vested in their contributions plus actual earnings thereon and are 20% vested in Employer match or discretionary contributions after one year with an additional 20% for each year thereafter until the participant is 100% vested.
