Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for safe harbor nonelective matching contributions equal to 150% of a participant's salary deferrals which do not exceed 1% of compensation, plus 50% of a participant's deferrals which exceed 1% but do not exceed 6% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become fully vested in Plan Sponsor safe harbor matching contributions after two years of service.
