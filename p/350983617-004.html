Match formula, as filed

The Employer makes a safe-harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 6% of compensation that each participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant becomes 25% vested after two years of service, increasing by 25% per additional year thereafter, with full vesting after five years of credited service.
