Match formula, as filed

The new matching contribution is compliant with Safe Harbor guidelines - all eligible participants will receive 100% of the first 3% of the "eligible" Participant's Compensation contributed to the Plan and 50% of the next 2% of the "eligible" Participant's Compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed
