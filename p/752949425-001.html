Match formula, as filed

In 2025, the Company matched 50% of employee pre-tax and/or Roth contributions, up to 6% of eligible compensation deferred to the Plan, resulting in a maximum matching contribution of 3% of eligible compensation, subject to the $350,000 annual compensation limit.

Vesting, as filed

Participants vest in Company employer matching contributions at the rate of 33% per year of service and are fully (100%) vested after three years of service.
