Match formula, as filed

The Plan has an Employer matching contribution of 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation contributed to the Plan, and 50% of the next 2% of eligible compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants hired on or after January 1, 2020, are 100% vested in safe harbor employer matching contributions and earnings thereon after two years of continuous service.
