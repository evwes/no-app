Match formula, as filed

The Company matches $0.50 for each $1.00 an employee defers on a pre-tax and/or Roth 401(k) post-tax basis up to the first 6% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant vests in the Company's contributions at the rate of 20% for each of the first four years of service and becomes 100% vested after completing the fifth year of service.
