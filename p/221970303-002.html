Match formula, as filed

The Company's matching contribution is 100% of the first 3%, and 50% of the next 2%, of compensation deferred by a Member in a plan year.

Vesting, as filed

Company matching contributions made for plan years commencing prior to January 1, 2000 were 20% vested after three years of service and continued to vest an additional 20% each year, becoming fully vested after the Member completed seven years of service.
