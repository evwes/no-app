Match formula, as filed

Eligible participants that are employees of Verotown are immediately eligible to receive employer matching contributions upon entering the Plan equal to 100% of the of the first 4.00% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 20% vested after one year of vested service and continues to vest in increments of 20% until 100% vested after five years of vested service.
