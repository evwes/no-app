Match formula, as filed

…Gible to participate in the Plan on or after January 1, 2008, but before July 1, 2016, UPS makes SavingsPlus matching contributions to each participant's account equal to 100% of pre-tax and/or Roth 401(k) contributions made to the Plan up to 3.5% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

For employees hired after December 31, 2024, the contribution is 100% vested after three years of service, or if earlier, age 62.
