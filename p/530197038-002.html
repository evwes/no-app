Match formula, as filed

Effective January 1, 2024, the Credit Union matched 100% of the first 5% of pre-tax contributions made by the employee.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 6% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

PENTAGON FEDERAL CREDIT UNION THRIFT/ The Company may also make a non-elective discretionary profit-sharing contribution, up to 6% of the Participant’s eligible compensation, to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after three years of credited service.
