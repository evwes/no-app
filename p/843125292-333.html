Match formula, as filed

The Hospital makes a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 1% of compensation contributed by participants plus 50% of the next 5% contributed (maximum match of 3.5% of compensation).

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 1.5% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

For the 2025 plan year, the Hospital made a discretionary non-elective contribution equal to 1.5% of compensation for each eligible participant.

Vesting, as filed

For participants who terminated prior to February 28, 2013, vesting in employer contribution accounts is based on years of credited service with 100% vesting attained after five years.
