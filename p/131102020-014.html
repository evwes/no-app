The Company makes a matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 6% of eligible earnings contributed by a participant (Safe Harbor Matching Contribution).

Vesting, as filed

The participant receives an additional 15% vesting credit upon the completion of each additional year of vesting service, thereby becoming 100% vested after completing five years of vesting service, as defined in the Plan document.
