Match formula, as filed

Employer Contributions – The Company makes a matching contribution equal to one dollar for each dollar of participant contributions not to exceed 5% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Matching contributions vested over a 3-year period at a rate of 33% after one year of service a participant completed, 67% after 2 years of service, and 100% after 3 years of service and additional annual contributions vested at the rate of 20% for each year of qualifying service.
