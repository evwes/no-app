Match formula, as filed

Employer Contributions Each payroll the Company provides a matching contribution of 100% on up to 4% of an employee’s compensation, as defined in the Plan document, for all eligible participants.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 3% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

In addition, each payroll the Company provides a non-elective retirement contribution of 3% of an employee's compensation, as defined in the Plan document, to all eligible employees who are not eligible to participate in the Company-sponsored defined benefit pension plans.
