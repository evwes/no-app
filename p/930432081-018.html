Match formula, as filed

The Company’s matching contribution is 100% of participant contributions for the first 3% of compensation, as defined by the Plan, and 50% of participant contributions on the next 5% of compensation up to 8% of compensation.

Vesting, as filed

These contributions will vest 100% after three years of vesting service.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Koch Companies Defined Contribution Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 97.4% of the trust's $13.4 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
