Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100% of the first 1% of eligible compensation, plus 50% of salary deferrals up to the next 5% of eligible compensation, for a maximum of 3.5% of eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after two years of credited service.
