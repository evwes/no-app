Match formula, as filed

The regular match is made each pay period and is 100 percent of the first 3 percent and 50 percent of the next 3 percent of pretax and after-tax compensation contributed to the plan by each participant.

Vesting, as filed

Generally, the RSP Plan participants become fully vested in employer matching, core, and discretionary contributions, plus earnings thereon, after two years of service with the Company.
