Match formula, as filed

The regular match is made each pay period and is 100 percent of the first 3 percent and 50 percent of the next 3 percent of pretax and after-tax compensation contributed to the plan by each participant.

Vesting, as filed

Generally, the RSP Plan participants become fully vested in employer matching, core, and discretionary contributions, plus earnings thereon, after two years of service with the Company.

Funds held by the master trust

This plan's money is pooled in Amrize Us Union 401(K) Plan And Amrize Us Rsp Master Trust. That trust files no fund-by-fund schedule of assets, but it does report its collective trust interests on Schedule D, and those are the funds below. The amounts are the trust's, not this plan's — every member plan shares them, so no per-plan or per-participant balance is public for these funds. They account for 95.1% of the trust's $1.6 billion; the rest is held in vehicles the trust does not itemise in its filing.
