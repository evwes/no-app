Match formula, as filed

Participating employers provide matching contributions of up to 6% of a participant's eligible pay.

Employer nonelective contribution, as filed

This plan contributes 6% of pay regardless of what a participant puts in — separate from any matching contribution.

Represented employees receive a non-elective contribution of 4-6%, based on age and service points.

Vesting, as filed

Employer contributions plus actual earnings thereon vest at a rate of 20% per year.
