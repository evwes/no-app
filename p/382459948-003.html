Match formula, as filed

The Plan provides for employer matching contributions of 50 percent of the first 4 percent of compensation deferred by the participant for a maximum of a 2 percent contribution.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are 100 percent vested in contributions made by MyMichigan Health after three years of credited service.
