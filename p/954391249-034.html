Match formula, as filed

The Plan Sponsor matches 50% of each participant’s payroll contribution, not to exceed $1,000 per year, prorated based on hours of service credited during the Plan year and if employed on the last day of the Plan year.

Vesting, as filed

In the event of Plan termination, participants would become 100% vested in their Plan Sponsor contributions.
