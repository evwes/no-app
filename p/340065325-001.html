Match formula, as filed

Under the Plan, the Corporation makes matching contributions each pay period equal to 100% of each participant’s elective contributions made during that pay period up to 6% of the participant’s eligible compensation.

Vesting, as filed

Participants are immediately vested in both their and the Corporation’s contributions, plus actual earnings thereon.
