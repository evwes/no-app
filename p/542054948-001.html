Match formula, as filed

The Plan was amended effective January 1, 2025, to add a safe harbor matching contribution equal to 100 percent of the first 3 percent of the participant's compensation contributed to the Plan and 50 percent of the next 2 percent of the participant's compensation contributed to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 30 percent vested after one year of credited service, 60 percent vested after two years of credited service and becomes 100 percent vested after three years of credited service.
