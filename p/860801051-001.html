Match formula, as filed

The Company contributes a Qualified Automatic Contribution Arrangement (QACA) safe harbor match of 100% of the first 1% of eligible compensation that a participant contributes, and 50% of the next 5% of eligible compensation a participant contributes to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

A participant is 100% vested after two years of credited service in the Company QACA safe harbor match.
