Match formula, as filed

The Company makes a matching contribution to each participant’s account equal to 100% of elective deferrals not exceeding 3% of the participant’s compensation, plus 50% of each participant’s elective deferrals in excess of 3%, but not in excess of 5%.

Vesting, as filed

The Company’s matching contributions vest based on years of service, which is a calendar plan year with 1,000 or more hours of service.
