Match formula, as filed

The Company matches participant contributions at a rate of 100% of the first 1% to 5% of the participant's qualifying compensation for each pay period.

Vesting, as filed

The vesting period is three years and is graded, which means Company contributions will vest one-third after each year of service until participants are fully vested after three years of service.
