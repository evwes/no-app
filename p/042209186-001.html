Match formula, as filed

The Company’s discretionary matching contribution is equal to 100% of the first 6% (5% for new participants entering the Plan on or after April 1, 2026) of eligible compensation for pre-tax and Roth participant contributions to the Plan.

Vesting, as filed

Participants become 100% vested in the Company’s matching contributions, plus any income or losses on those balances, after two years of service.
